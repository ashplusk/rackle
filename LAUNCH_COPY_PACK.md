### Version A: Warm and direct

Hi [First Name],

I’m building Rackle, a daily Charleston decision game for NMJL American Mahjong players.

Think Wordle, but for the Charleston.

Each day, players get the same starting rack, make three passes, and receive a Rackle IQ based on their table read. They can then compare scores on a Global board or a club board.

I’m inviting a small group of founding clubs to try v1 before we open it more broadly.

The goal is simple:

- Give your players a fun daily Mahjong ritual
- Create light competition inside your group
- Help us find bugs, polish the experience, and shape what comes next

No pressure. No big rollout. Just a small group of players testing it, sharing feedback, and seeing if it becomes something your club enjoys using daily.

Would you be open to trying Rackle with your group?

## 11. Short blurbs for club owners

### What is Rackle?

Rackle is a daily Charleston decision game for NMJL American Mahjong players. Players open the same rack, make three passes, receive a Rackle IQ, and compare their table read with friends or their club.

### Why clubs like it

Rackle gives Mahjong groups a quick daily ritual between games. It creates friendly competition, sparks discussion, and helps players compare how they read the same rack.

### Founding club ask

Founding clubs help shape Rackle before a broader launch. The ask is simple: invite a few players, try the daily rack, share scores, and send honest feedback.
